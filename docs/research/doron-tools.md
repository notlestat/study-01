# Doron Supply tools research for STUDY/01

Research date: 13 October 2026. Source boundary: the official [Doron Supply shop](https://www.doronsupply.com/shop) and linked official product pages. Product publication/update dates were generally absent; crawl dates ranged from today to several months ago. This is a current retrieval of public descriptions, not proof that a downloaded product matches them.

## Evidence and scope

- FACT: Corey stated that Doron's paid tools are not installed because they cost money. Local package searches stopped after that clarification. No purchases or downloads were made.
- OBSERVATION: The accessible shop lists 22 functional plugin/action/template products, plus one brush asset. This inventory includes related free tools, even where the shop does not tag them as `Tools`. Fonts, garment mockups, bundles and texture-only packs are outside the functional-tool matrix.
- FACT ABOUT DOCUMENTATION: Every feature below is a vendor-documented workflow. None of the paid algorithms, Photoshop action sequences or PSD layer graphs was available for reverse engineering. A public description is not runtime evidence.
- UNKNOWN: Actual defaults, exact maths, action order, filter parameters, performance, export fidelity, licence permissions and compatibility beyond the explicit statements on the pages.
- REA: An existing texture-scan ZIP was inventoried earlier. Its evidence does not establish the internals of any tool in this matrix. No new native/code target was bound for the expanded catalog pass.
- Agent Reach search/web routing was attempted. Exa negotiation and Jina DNS failed, so the available web tool retrieved the official pages. No account login, subscription, asset checkout or external write was performed.
- Official product pages contain links to demos. Video playback and transcripts were not inspected; descriptions of behaviour are attributed to product pages, not claimed as observed demonstrations.

`Unavailable` in the REA column means no corresponding package/runtime was supplied or discovered in the bounded search before the user clarified that the paid tools were absent. It does not claim to search the entire machine.

- CHROMETONE's description says 14 preset colour combinations while the features list says 16 preset looks. This report does not resolve which ships.
- CRT Phaser is categorised as Plugin but its contents and description say Photoshop actions/ATN. This is not evidence of a native plugin.
- Printer Noise's description mentions PNGs and patterns while its contents list TIF textures. The analogous Mixed Media page says 50+ textures in prose and 100+ TIFs in contents. These texture packs were not acquired.
- Angelic Grunge has no accessible descriptive workflow. Pixel Perfect's page is a short older-style page. These remain partial entries, not inferred mechanisms.
- Source popularity, client-credit, exclusivity, quality and patent claims were not used as evidence of capability or suitability.

## Two future mechanisms with the strongest fit

### Editable displacement field

INFERENCE FOR STUDY: mobChain's useful interaction is the linked, editable map and independent X/Y strength, not a named preset. Build an original displacement field from a user-selected material image or a fresh seeded field. Keep it independent of composition geometry; preview without commit, retain it as a recipe, target image or type, and expose source/processed comparison. Exact map channel interpretation, sampling and edge treatment would be STUDY design choices, not claimed Doron reconstruction.

Public basis: [mobChain's documented map/reference-layer/X–Y workflow](https://www.doronsupply.com/product/mobchain-plugin).

Benchmark review: compare unprocessed and field-processed versions across ORDER, SILENCE and TENSION, photographs and flat graphics. Review whether the displacement changes the image/type relationship, preserves deliberate quiet areas, and behaves differently across material scales. Avoid calling uniform jitter a new composition.

### Editable ink separation and material loss

INFERENCE FOR STUDY: combine original tonal separation, ordered or error-diffusion treatment, editable ink offsets and per-element material masks. Keep source type editable, with bleed/erosion as reversible appearance. Use existing colour/processing architecture where possible so it does not produce several overlapping control families.

Public basis: [DEPTHTONE's independently editable shadow/midtone/highlight separations and registration](https://www.doronsupply.com/product/depthtone-classic), [DITHERTONE's palette/scale/angle and separated-mask workflow](https://www.doronsupply.com/product/dithertone-pro), and [Ink Bleed's editable-text/modular-effect description](https://www.doronsupply.com/product/ink-bleed-actions).

Benchmark review: hold composition constant to judge dot structure, tonal separation, alpha edges and registration first; then compare across the frozen diverse compositions. Look for meaningful light/dark structure and spatial ink relationships rather than a blanket grain overlay. Print-readiness must be separately validated before making that claim.

These are proposals, not implemented features. Direct canvas transforms and user-selected material layers are the current implementation priority. This research adds no vendor code, patterns, LUTs, brush shapes or built-in Doron artwork to STUDY.
